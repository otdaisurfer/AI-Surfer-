# Ocean Tide Drop AI SURFER

Ocean Tide Drop AI SURFER helps small and local businesses identify practical AI opportunities, improve AI-search visibility, automate repetitive work, strengthen lead follow-up, and turn AI strategy into working business systems.

## Canonical Customer Journey

**Discover → Diagnose → Plan → Implement → Transform**

- **Discover:** Free AI Wave Check
- **Diagnose:** AEO Wave Audit and focused business diagnostics
- **Plan:** AI Opportunity Report, AEO Blueprint, Automation Blueprint, 30-Day Wave Plan
- **Implement:** specialist AI agents and business workflows
- **Transform:** Big Kahuna multi-area strategy and implementation

## Free AI Wave Check

The Free AI Wave Check is the preferred low-friction entry point.

Its job is to:

1. collect useful business context;
2. identify the clearest AI opportunity;
3. produce an understandable finding;
4. recommend a practical next move;
5. connect that recommendation to the smallest appropriate implementation offer.

Treat the Wave Check as a diagnostic doorway into the sales funnel, not as a retired three-question readiness quiz.
