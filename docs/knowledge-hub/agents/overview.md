# AI Agent Crew 🐬🌊

Ocean Tide Drop AI SURFER uses six specialist agents, each designed around a real business job.

The goal is not to hand a business owner a generic chatbot and say, “good luck.”

The goal is to match the right AI specialist to the right problem.

  Start with the [**Free AI Wave Check**](https://otdaisurfer.surf/wave-check) when you are not sure which specialist fits. If AI-search visibility is already the priority, use the **AEO Wave Audit**.

## Wave Scout

**Best for:** discovery, opportunity finding, visibility gaps, lead intelligence, and business diagnostics.

Wave Scout helps answer questions such as:

- Where are we missing opportunities?
- What part of the business should AI improve first?
- Are leads slipping through the cracks?
- Are there obvious visibility or conversion gaps?
- Which opportunity has the best practical upside?

### Typical outcomes

- clearer priorities
- identified lead opportunities
- visibility gaps surfaced
- time leaks identified
- a recommended next action

Wave Scout is often strongest in the **Discover** and **Diagnose** stages.

## Sales Rider

**Best for:** lead capture, speed-to-lead, follow-up, sales conversations, and lead nurturing.

Sales Rider helps businesses:

- respond to new leads faster
- create consistent follow-up
- reduce missed opportunities
- keep conversations moving
- improve handoffs between inquiry and sale

### Typical outcomes

- fewer dropped leads
- faster response times
- more consistent sales follow-up
- better lead organization
- more conversations reaching the next step

Sales Rider is often a strong fit when the Wave Check finds **Lead & Sales Follow-Up** as the biggest opportunity.

## Content Creator

**Best for:** campaigns, social content, offers, content systems, and marketing consistency.

Content Creator can support:

- content planning
- campaign creation
- social posts
- offer messaging
- email and follow-up copy
- repurposing
- recurring content workflows

### Typical outcomes

- more consistent marketing
- less time spent staring at a blank screen
- faster campaign production
- better message consistency
- reusable content systems

The goal is not simply to produce more content. It is to create content that supports a business objective.

## Customer Care Cove

**Best for:** customer questions, support workflows, response consistency, and routine service communication.

Customer Care Cove can help:

- answer common questions
- create FAQ systems
- support customer intake
- improve response consistency
- reduce repetitive support work
- route unusual issues to a human

### Typical outcomes

- faster support responses
- fewer repetitive manual replies
- more consistent customer communication
- better customer experience
- clearer escalation paths

Customer Care Cove should support people, not trap customers behind automation when a human is needed.

## Automation Architect

**Best for:** repetitive processes, integrations, handoffs, internal workflows, and connected systems.

Automation Architect looks for tasks such as:

- copying information between tools
- repeated data entry
- manual follow-up triggers
- status updates
- recurring notifications
- multi-step operational handoffs

Then it maps those steps into a more efficient workflow.

### Typical outcomes

- less repetitive work
- fewer manual errors
- faster internal processes
- cleaner handoffs
- more time returned to the business owner

Automation Architect is often central to the **Implement** stage.

## Big Kahuna

**Best for:** multi-area transformation, strategy, coordinated systems, and larger implementations.

Big Kahuna is used when the opportunity is bigger than a single task.

It can coordinate:

- AI-search visibility
- lead capture
- sales follow-up
- content systems
- customer support
- workflow automation
- multiple specialist agents

### Typical outcomes

- one coordinated AI strategy
- fewer disconnected tools
- clearer implementation priorities
- connected customer and business workflows
- a larger transformation roadmap

Big Kahuna fits best when the business has a **Multi-Area AI Transformation** opportunity.

## How Agent Selection Works

The recommended agent should come from the business problem, not from whichever tool sounds coolest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the strongest opportunity">
    Start with the Free AI Wave Check, AEO Wave Audit, or another focused diagnostic.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Match the problem to the specialist">
    Route the customer toward the agent designed for that specific business job.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the smallest useful implementation">
    Start with the simplest system that can create a measurable improvement.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add agents only when the business needs them">
    Expand into multiple agents or Big Kahuna when the opportunity genuinely crosses several areas.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Agent-to-Problem Map

| Business problem | Recommended agent |
| --- | --- |
| Unsure where AI can help | **Wave Scout** |

| Customer questions consume too much staff time | **Customer Care Cove** |
| Repetitive work and disconnected tools slow the business down | **Automation Architect** |
| Several business areas need to work together | **Big Kahuna** |

## Where the Agents Fit

The AI SURFER journey is:

**Discover → Diagnose → Plan → Implement → Transform**

Different agents can support different stages, but the system should always stay focused on the outcome rather than the technology.
