# AI Agent Crew

AI SURFER uses six named specialist agents. Each agent is tied to a business problem instead of being presented as an abstract AI persona.

## Wave Scout

Finds overlooked AI opportunities, visibility gaps, and qualified lead opportunities.

## Sales Rider

Helps businesses respond to leads faster, organize follow-up, and move more conversations toward a sale.

## Content Creator

Builds repeatable AI-assisted content systems so marketing stays consistent without consuming the entire week.

## Customer Care Cove

Creates faster, more consistent answers and workflows for routine customer questions and service requests.

## Automation Architect

Maps repetitive tasks, integrations, and handoffs into connected AI-powered workflows.

## Big Kahuna

Combines strategy, visibility, automation, agents, workflows, and growth opportunities into a coordinated transformation plan.

## Agent Selection

The Wave Check or Audit should recommend the agent that best matches the strongest problem area. Multi-area opportunities can route to Big Kahuna.
