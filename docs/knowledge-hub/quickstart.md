# Quickstart

The fastest way to understand AI SURFER is to follow the same path a customer takes.

## 1. Run the Free AI Wave Check

Use the Wave Check to identify the clearest AI opportunity in the business. It should gather lightweight business context, produce an understandable finding, and recommend a practical next move.

## 2. Review the AI Wave Report

The report should explain:

- the strongest opportunity;
- why it matters;
- the priority actions;
- the recommended specialist agent or implementation path;
- a simple 30-day plan.

## 3. Choose the Smallest Useful Implementation

Use the primary public ladder:

- **Wave Starter — $497** for one focused implementation sprint.
- **Wave Builder — $1,997** for connected visibility, lead-flow, follow-up, or automation systems.
- **Tsunami Growth — $3,997** for multi-area strategy and deeper implementation.

## 4. Move Into Delivery

Depending on the finding, implementation may use one or more of the AI SURFER specialist agents:

Wave Scout, Sales Rider, Content Creator, Customer Care Cove, Automation Architect, or Big Kahuna.

## 5. Measure the Result

Every implementation should connect to a business outcome such as faster follow-up, less manual work, stronger AI-search visibility, more consistent content, improved support response, or better lead conversion.
