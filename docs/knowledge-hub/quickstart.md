# Quickstart 🌊

The fastest way to understand Ocean Tide Drop AI SURFER is to follow the same path a customer takes:

**Wave Check → Wave Report → Choose Your Wave → Implement → Measure**

You do not need to understand every AI tool before you begin. Start with the business problem, identify the best opportunity, then build the smallest useful solution.

<Tip>
  If you are new to AI SURFER, begin with the **Free AI Wave Check**. It is the front door to the entire system.
</Tip>

## Step 1: Take the Free AI Wave Check

The AI Wave Check gathers a small amount of business context and looks for practical opportunities in areas such as:

- lead generation
- sales follow-up
- customer support
- repetitive admin work
- content production
- AI-search visibility
- workflow automation

The goal is not to overwhelm you with technology. The goal is to answer one question:

**Where can AI create the most useful business improvement first?**

## Step 2: Review Your AI Wave Report

Your Wave Report turns the assessment into a clear action path.

A strong report should show:

- your **AI Opportunity Score**
- your **Biggest Wave**
- what the finding means for your business
- the most important actions to take
- the recommended specialist AI agent
- a practical next step
- a simple 30-day direction when appropriate

The report should make the next decision easier, not more complicated.

## Step 5: Implement the Smallest Useful System

AI SURFER follows a simple rule:

**Do not automate everything. Automate what matters first.**

A first implementation might be:

- an automated lead follow-up sequence
- an AI customer support workflow
- an AEO/GEO visibility improvement
- a lead capture system
- a content production workflow
- a recurring admin automation
- a connected sales process

The first build should solve a real business problem and create a result that can be measured.

## Step 6: Measure the Result

Every implementation should connect to a business outcome.

Examples include:

- faster response time
- fewer missed leads
- less repetitive manual work
- stronger AI-search visibility
- more consistent content
- improved customer support
- better lead conversion
- time returned to the business owner

Once the first system is working, AI SURFER can identify the next best wave.
