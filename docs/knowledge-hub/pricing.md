# Pricing and Implementation Offers

The canonical public implementation ladder is:

| Offer | Price | Best fit | Primary CTA |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| **Wave Starter** | **$497** | One focused AI implementation sprint | Buy Wave Starter |
| **Wave Builder** | **$1,997** | Connected visibility, lead flow, follow-up, or automation systems | Request a Strategy Call |
| **Tsunami Growth** | **$3,997** | Multi-area AI strategy and deeper implementation | Talk With AI Surfer |

## Wave Starter — $497

A focused sprint to turn one clear AI opportunity into a working business system.

## Wave Builder — $1,997

A larger build for businesses ready to connect AI visibility, lead flow, follow-up, and automation into one practical growth system.

## Tsunami Growth — $3,997

Strategy plus deeper implementation for businesses that need multiple AI systems working together across the customer journey.

## Choosing an Offer

If a visitor is unsure which wave fits, the preferred next step is the **Free AI Wave Check**. The funnel should recommend the smallest useful implementation rather than pushing a larger package by default.

## Documentation Rule

Do not present older tier names, inactive membership pricing, or legacy Done-For-You pricing as the current public implementation ladder when they conflict with the three offers above.
