# AEO and GEO

AI SURFER treats **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** and **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** as practical business visibility work.

## Core Questions

A useful AEO/GEO review asks:

- Can AI assistants understand what the business is?
- Can they identify its services, products, location, expertise, and trust signals?
- Does the website answer the real questions buyers ask?
- Is important business information structured consistently?
- Are proof points and authoritative references easy to discover?
- Are there clear citation opportunities?

## Business Outcome

The goal is not simply to "rank in AI." The goal is to make the business easier for AI systems and buyers to understand, trust, summarize, and act on.

## AI SURFER AEO Dimensions

- Conversational Intent
- Entity Authority
- Technical Readiness
- Content Authority
- AI Visibility
- Citation Opportunities
