# AEO Wave Audit

The **AEO Wave Audit** is a deeper assessment of a business's readiness to be understood, surfaced, and cited by AI-powered search and answer engines.

## What the Audit Evaluates

The audit can evaluate:

- **Conversational Intent** — whether content answers the questions buyers actually ask.
- **Entity Authority** — whether the business, services, people, and locations are clearly identified.
- **Technical Readiness** — whether the site exposes useful machine-readable structure and crawlable information.
- **Content Authority** — whether the business demonstrates useful expertise and evidence.
- **AI Visibility** — whether important information is discoverable and answer-ready.
- **Citation Opportunities** — where clearer proof, structure, or authoritative references could improve answer-engine confidence.

## Output

The audit should prioritize action instead of dumping raw findings. A useful report explains:

- current score or readiness;
- biggest visibility gap;
- the most important corrective actions;
- the first implementation to test;
- the next measurement point.

## Positioning

AEO is the front door. AI SURFER connects AEO and GEO directly to discoverability, trust, qualified demand, and revenue rather than presenting them as abstract SEO terminology.
