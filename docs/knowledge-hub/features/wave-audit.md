# AEO Wave Audit 🌊🔎

The **AEO Wave Audit** is a deeper visibility assessment for businesses that want to understand how well they are positioned for AI-powered search.

It looks beyond traditional rankings and asks a more useful question:

**Can AI systems clearly understand who you are, what you offer, why you are credible, and when to surface your business in an answer?**

<Tip>
  AEO is the front door. The goal is not to chase jargon. The goal is to make your business easier for AI search systems to understand, trust, and reference.
</Tip>

## How the Audit Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your digital footprint">
    AI SURFER examines the business website, important pages, service information, and the signals that help define the business online.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Score AEO readiness">
    The audit evaluates the six core dimensions and identifies where visibility is strongest and where the biggest gaps remain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the highest-value gap">
    Instead of treating every issue equally, the audit highlights the problem most likely to affect discoverability, trust, or qualified demand.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn findings into action">
    The report prioritizes the first corrections and the first implementation worth testing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You Receive

A useful AEO Wave Audit should give you a clear decision path, not a pile of technical notes.

Your report can include:

- an overall **AEO readiness score**
- scores across the six audit dimensions
- your **biggest visibility gap**
- the reasons that gap matters
- the most important corrective actions
- the first implementation to test
- the next measurement point
- a practical AEO/GEO roadmap

## What We Look For

Depending on the business, the audit may review things such as:

- whether service pages answer buyer questions directly
- whether business entities are clearly named and connected
- whether key facts are consistent across pages
- whether structured data can improve machine understanding
- whether proof, examples, testimonials, or source signals are strong enough
- whether content is specific enough to be useful in an AI-generated answer
- whether important pages are easy to crawl and interpret
- whether there are obvious citation and authority gaps

## AEO vs. GEO

AEO and GEO are closely related, but they focus on slightly different parts of the same visibility challenge.

**AEO, Answer Engine Optimization,** focuses on making information easy for answer systems to understand and use.

**GEO, Generative Engine Optimization,** focuses more broadly on improving the chance that generative AI systems recognize, reference, and surface a business or its content.

AI SURFER treats them as connected disciplines rather than separate buzzwords.

## What Happens After the Audit

The audit is designed to lead into implementation.

Depending on the findings, the next step may involve:

- rewriting or restructuring important pages
- strengthening entity signals
- adding or improving structured data
- creating answer-ready content
- improving proof and authority signals
- fixing crawlability or technical visibility issues
- building a content or citation strategy
- connecting visibility work with lead capture and follow-up

## The Goal

The goal is not simply to make a website more "optimized."

The goal is to help the business become:

- easier to understand
- easier to trust
- easier to surface
- easier to cite
- easier for the right customer to discover
