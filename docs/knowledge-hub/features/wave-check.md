# Free AI Wave Check 🌊

The **Free AI Wave Check** is the front door to Ocean Tide Drop AI SURFER.

It is designed for business owners who know AI could help, but do not want to waste time guessing which tool, automation, or strategy to start with.

The Wave Check looks at a few key parts of the business, identifies the strongest opportunity, and turns that into a practical next step.

<Tip>
  The goal is not to give you more AI homework. The goal is to show you where AI can make the biggest useful difference first.
</Tip>

## What the Wave Check Answers

The Wave Check is built around one core question:

**Where is the clearest practical AI opportunity in your business right now?**

That opportunity may be hiding in sales follow-up, customer support, visibility, repetitive work, content production, or several areas at once.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell us about your business">
    Share lightweight context about how your business operates, where leads come from, and where work tends to slow down.
  </Step>

  <Step title="AI SURFER identifies the strongest opportunity">
    The system looks for practical gaps where AI, automation, or a specialist agent could create a measurable improvement.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive your AI Wave Report">
    Your results summarize the opportunity, explain why it matters, and recommend a next move.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the smallest useful implementation">
    If a paid build makes sense, the recommendation connects you to the right AI SURFER offer rather than pushing you into a larger package than you need.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You Get

Your **AI Wave Report** is designed to be easy to understand and useful immediately.

Depending on the result, it can include:

- an **AI Opportunity Score**
- your **Biggest Wave**
- a plain-English explanation of the finding
- the business problem behind the opportunity
- recommended priority actions
- the best-fit specialist AI agent
- a practical next step
- a simple 30-day direction when appropriate

The result should answer **what to work on first and why**.
