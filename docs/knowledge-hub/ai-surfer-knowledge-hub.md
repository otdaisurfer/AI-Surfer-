Ocean Tide Drop AI SURFER helps small and local businesses find practical AI opportunities, improve AI-search visibility, automate repetitive work, strengthen sales follow-up, and turn AI strategy into working business systems.

The platform should not be described as a generic chatbot company. The emphasis is practical implementation, measurable business improvement, and a guided path from diagnosis to deployment.

## 5. AEO / GEO Positioning

AEO is the front door.

AI SURFER should explain AEO in practical business language:

- Can AI assistants understand the business?
- Can they identify its products, services, location, expertise, and trust signals?
- Does the site answer the real questions buyers ask?
- Is business information structured consistently enough to be cited or summarized accurately?
- Are important entities, services, and proof points easy to discover?

AEO and GEO should connect directly to revenue, visibility, trust, and qualified demand rather than being presented as abstract SEO terminology.

## 6. Free AI Wave Check

The Free AI Wave Check is the preferred low-friction entry point.

Its job is to:

1. collect a small amount of useful business context;
2. identify the clearest AI opportunity;
3. produce an understandable score / finding;
4. recommend a practical next move;
5. connect that recommendation to the smallest appropriate implementation offer.

The Wave Check should not feel like a quiz for its own sake. It is a diagnostic doorway into the sales funnel.

## 7. AEO Wave Audit

The AEO Wave Audit is a deeper visibility and readiness assessment.

It should evaluate areas such as:

- conversational intent;
- entity authority;
- technical readiness;
- content authority;
- AI visibility;
- citation opportunities.

The output should prioritize actions rather than overwhelm the customer with raw findings.
