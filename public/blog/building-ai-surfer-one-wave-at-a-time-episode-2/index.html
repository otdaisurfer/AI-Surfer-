Building AI SURFER · Episode 2

Giving the Dream a Name

By Shannon · November 16, 2026

In the first episode, I introduced the life behind the logo. This part is about the dream that keeps bringing me back to the work: Ocean Tide Drop AI SURFER.

For about a year and a half, I’ve been teaching myself AI, using free tools and resources and figuring things out as I go. One app, one question, one “why won’t this work?” at a time.

There’s a lot of learning tucked into that name. And there’s still a lot ahead.

A name to build around

Ocean Tide Drop AI SURFER gives this work a home. The website, the ideas, the products, the videos—they’re pieces of a business I’m trying to bring together.

“Ride the Wave 🌊 Grow with AI.” That’s the invitation. You can begin with a question and learn the next piece as you need it. That’s how I’ve been doing it.

Now there’s AI Fin, our dolphin, and the Wave Handler with her laptop and surfboard. There are turquoise and coral colors, and a whole ocean of ideas. I love the creative part: giving something I’ve imagined a shape I can actually see.

The work behind the picture

Then comes the part where I have to make the pieces work together.

A page can look finished and still need another pass. A connection can take longer than I expected. I can spend time learning one thing, then discover there’s another thing I need to understand before I can use it.

I’ve had plenty of do-overs. I’m learning to give myself a smaller job: work on one piece, check it, and then take the next step. Some days, that one piece takes all the patience I have.

Who I’m building this for

As I learn, I keep coming back to small business owners. People who have customers to help, messages to answer, and a business to run while they’re trying to understand what AI could actually do for them.

My hope is to turn what I’m learning into practical help. Start with the business and its biggest challenge. Find one useful place to begin. Then work from there.

That’s the thinking behind the Free AI Wave Check. A starting point can make a big, confusing subject feel a little more manageable.

Still learning. Still building.

Giving the dream a name didn’t finish the work. It gave me something to keep building.

I’m sharing this while it’s still unfolding. There are ideas I’m excited about, things I’m still figuring out, and lessons I’ll carry into whatever I try next.

If you’re learning something new while trying to build a better future for yourself, you’re welcome here. Come learn with me.

My life. My lessons. One wave at a time.
